    Lake analysis

    Rank lakes by average wind speed — for example, find the calmest lakes in a state. Uses the date range, months, and temperature/area filters from Controls above. Wind data is fetched for every lake in batches, so this fits long, monthly-style periods.

    Data: Open-Meteo (ERA5 reanalysis, interpolated to lake coordinates) or Meteostat (weather-station observations, interpolated to lake coordinates). Lake locations: USGS GNIS. Lake areas: USGS NHD.
